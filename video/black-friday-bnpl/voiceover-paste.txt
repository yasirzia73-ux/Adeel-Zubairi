This Friday, Americans are expected to spend twelve point nine billion dollars online. In ONE day.

And about eight hundred million dollars of that won't be paid. Not yet.

It's called Buy Now, Pay Later. Split your purchase into four easy payments. Zero interest. Sounds perfect, right?

Here's the trap. Stores love it, because people spend more when the price feels smaller. A four-hundred-dollar TV becomes "just one hundred dollars." And your brain only hears one hundred.

Then it stacks. A TV here. Shoes there. A gift for your mom. Suddenly you have six payments, due on six different dates.

Miss one, and you can get hit with late fees. And some plans can even hurt your credit score.

So here's the rule. If you can't afford it in one payment, you can't afford it in four.

Only use Buy Now, Pay Later if the money is already sitting in your account.
