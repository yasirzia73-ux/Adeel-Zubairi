GTA 6 just made three point seven nine billion dollars. In ONE day.

That's not a prediction. Take-Two says it's a new record for any kind of entertainment. Ever.

For comparison, GTA Five made about eight hundred million dollars on its first day. GTA Six made almost five times that.

It also sold thirty-two and a half million copies before it even came out.

So the company's stock must have exploded, right?

Wrong. On launch day, Take-Two's stock went down.

How? Remember what I told you: buy the rumor, sell the news. The stock jumped six percent the day before, after the game scored a ninety-nine on Metacritic. Then, on launch day, investors cashed out. The good news was already priced in.

The lesson: a record-breaking product doesn't always mean a record-breaking stock. At least, not right away.
