## Recording tips
- **Mic:** a phone voice memo works. Hold it about a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side, so your breath doesn't pop.
- **Room:** a small room with soft things (a closet, a bedroom with curtains and a bed) sounds much better than an empty room. Turn off fans and the AC.
- **Takes:** record one section at a time. If you stumble, pause, then repeat the whole sentence. Mistakes are easy to cut later.
- **Energy:** smile slightly while talking; it shows in your voice. Keep the pace steady and don't rush the numbers.
- **File:** export as WAV or a high-quality MP3. One file for the whole video is fine.
